Dimension calculations

PCB length from LED count

L = N × p

The selected LED count multiplied by the centre-to-centre pitch.

LED count from PCB length

N = floor(L ÷ p)

Only complete pitch positions are counted.

Unused PCB length

Lunused = L − N × p

The length remaining after fitting the calculated whole LED count.

LED position

dk = d0 + (k − 1) × p

Distance from the injection point to LED k, where k = 1…N.

Copper resistance

Temperature-adjusted resistivity

ρ(T) = 1.724×10−8 × [1 + 0.00393 × (T − 20)]

Copper resistivity in Ω·m, referenced to 20 °C.

Effective rail area

A = w × t × nCu

Cross-sectional area of one polarity, including parallel layers.

Resistance per pitch

RP = ρ(T) × p ÷ A

Resistance of one positive or return rail segment.

Resistance before first LED

R0 = ρ(T) × d0 ÷ A

Resistance of one polarity from injection to the first load.

Current and voltage

Strip and panel current

ISTRIP = N × IL IPANEL = NS × ISTRIP

Common-feed drop and top voltage

VFEED = IPANEL × RF VTOP = VS − VFEED

Voltage at LED k

Vk = VTOP − 2ISTRIPR0 − 2ILRPΣj

For LED k, Σj = (k − 1)(2N − k) ÷ 2. The factor 2 includes positive and return rails.

Current after LED k

Iafter,k = (N − k) × IL

Each successive segment carries one fewer downstream load.

Voltage drops

VPCB drop = VTOP − VN VTOTAL drop = VS − VN

Maximum current density

JMAX = ISTRIP ÷ A

Maximum occurs at the injected end of each strip.

Power loss and performance result

Copper loss in all strips

PCu = NS[2ISTRIP2R0 + 2IL2RPΣj2]

Σj² = (N − 1)N(2N − 1) ÷ 6, covering all pitch segments.

Common-feed loss

PFEED = IPANEL2 × RF

PASS / FAIL rule

PASS when VN ≥ VMIN FAIL when VN < VMIN

VN is the voltage at the lowest, most distant LED.

Engineering note: This is a DC resistive estimate for identical, uniformly spaced loads and equal positive/return copper. Validate multiplexed peak current, connector ratings, vias, thermal rise and actual PCB geometry separately.
